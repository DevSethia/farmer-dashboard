Frequently Asked Questions (FAQ)
Q: How often is satellite data refreshed for my farm?
A: Satellite imagery (NDVI index) is updated every 5 days using ISRO EOS-04 and Sentinel data passes.
Q: How do I link my Soil Health Card to GeoAgri?
A: Enter your 12-digit SHC Number in the Soil Health tab sidebar to auto-sync your soil parameters.
Q: Are the market prices updated in real-time?
A: Yes, e-NAM market rates update live during official APMC Mandi operating hours across states.
